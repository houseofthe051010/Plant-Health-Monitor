# CAD files

The full Fusion 360 assembly and assembly STEP are in [`Source CAD`](Source%20CAD). The printable meshes are in [`Individual STLs`](Individual%20STLs), and matching part-level STEP files are in [`Individual STEP files`](Individual%20STEP%20files).

## Print settings

- Printer: Anycubic Kobra S1
- Material: PLA
- Infill: 20%
- Walls: 2
